# Moneda Mundial 2.0 — Partner Outreach / English Brief
**Updated 17 October 2026. PRE-INCORPORATION, pre-launch, no customer funds handled.**

**Project:** Moneda Mundial 2.0 is an early-stage multilingual international remittance mobile-platform concept, currently in demo. We are seeking an appropriately regulated banking, transfer, and local-payout partner to assess a first cross-border remittance corridor from the United States to Honduras, subject to confirmed legal coverage and commercial feasibility.

**Desired model:** A regulated provider handles covered payment services, safeguarding/settlement and agreed compliance duties. Moneda Mundial would offer a compliant customer experience once contracted and legally approved. Services of interest include funding from eligible bank accounts/debit cards; bank deposits; and in-person cash pickup without a smartphone or app on the recipient's part (if legally offered in the corridor). The agent must perform beneficiary identification and online payout authorization. Nicaragua and all other countries remain in the global design catalog. Expansion to other Central and South American markets would be corridor-by-corridor after authorization.

**Current state:** React Native app, demo wallet and remittance states, simulated single-use pickup codes, and a server-only mock provider adapter with signed *mock* webhooks. **There is no licensed operating entity, executed provider contract, live payment API, actual stored funds, or real money movement.** No wallet or jurisdiction is represented as approved.

**Request for information (RFI):**
1. Can your corporate team assess US-origin consumer remittances to Honduras and verify which legal entities and regulated services cover both ends?
2. Can you confirm *bank credit* and *cash pickup without a recipient smartphone*, supported currencies, ID requirements, agent coverage and code-redemption process for this specific corridor?
3. Which onboarding documents, incorporated-entity information, business plans, beneficial ownership and compliance policies must be in place before sandbox access and production?
4. Which party takes responsibility for MSB/money-transmitter licensing (as applicable), KYC/AML/OFAC, customer funds, complaints, remittance disclosures, cancellation and refund requirements?
5. Do you offer official sandbox credentials and documentation for quotes, transfer submissions, signed webhooks, payouts, idempotency, reconciliation, reversals and cash pickup validation? Please provide technical documentation through your secure developer process.
6. What are the expected implementation, FX, settlement, refund, prefunding, minimum-volume and support costs?

**Next action requested:** Introductory business-development/partnership call and written feedback to the RFI. We will not represent the platform as licensed, operational or contracted pending approvals.

**Confidentiality:** This overview contains no personal ID, bank credentials or customer information. Any KYC/KYB documents must be exchanged only through the provider's secured onboarding channel.
